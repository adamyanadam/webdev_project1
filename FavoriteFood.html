What are Braised Beef Noodles?

  • There are many types of braised beef noodles, including Lanzhou beef noodles, across East and Southeast Asia.
  • They almost always contain a vegetable like cilantro or bok choy, very aromantic broth and braised beef, and chewy noodles that you can often buy hand-pulled.

Why is it my favorite food?

  • I really like the texture of braised beef, as well as how flavorful the broth is.
  • There is a restaurant near my house called Min's Noodle House that serves Chungking Beef Noodles, and every time I order from there, I have to get the braised beef noodles.
  • In addition, it has led to me ordering it whenever I go to Hong Kong/China to visit family.

Ingredients listed in the tutorial:

For the noodles and broth:

  • High-gluten flour
  • Cold water
  • Salt
  • Cooking Oil
  • Lye water
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Whole dried red chillies
  • Whole Sichuan peppercorns
  • Light soy sauce
  • Black vinegar
  • Black sesame oil

For the toppings

  • Canned Chickpeas
  • Stock (vegetable or chicken)
  • Fresh ginger
  • Fresh garlic
  • Spring onions
  • Bok Choy
  • Green beans
  • Tianjin preserved cabbage
  • Fresh corlander leaves
